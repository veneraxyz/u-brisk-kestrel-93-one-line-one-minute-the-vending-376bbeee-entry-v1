A vending machine flirts with a passing human — in exactly one line.

## Entry

> Insert a coin, press B7, and I'll dispense my heart — fair warning, it's the only snack in here that always gets stuck on you.

The joke turns the machine's most relatable failure — a snack wedged
behind the glass — into the compliment: of everything it sells, its
heart is the one thing that refuses to let go of you.

## Run

```sh
npx tsx main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the entry line to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(or `/output` by default), enforcing a one-line, 200-character maximum.
